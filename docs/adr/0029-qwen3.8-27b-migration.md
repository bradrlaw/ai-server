# ADR-0029: Migrate the `coding` and `big` slots to Qwen3.8-27B

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-15 (written up 2026-10-15)
- **Deciders:** @bradrlaw (+ Copilot CLI)
- **Supersedes:** [ADR-0008](0008-primary-coding-model-qwen3.6-27b.md) for the dense coding model
  only. Its Qwen3.6-35B-A3B MoE choice for `chat` still stands.

## Context
ADR-0008 put Qwen3.6-27B dense on `coding` (Q6_K, V100 idx1) and `big` (dual-V100
`-sm layer`). Qwen3.8-27B (`unsloth/Qwen3.8-27B-GGUF`) has the same `qwen35` architecture,
with the MTP `nextn` head embedded in the main GGUF. That makes it a drop-in on our
llama.cpp build 9850 (no rebuild), and `--spec-type draft-mtp` works unchanged.

The swap shipped in PR #46 (branch `qwen3.8-27b-migration`, merged 2026-08-24 together with
the `fast` → `small` rename in PR #48) without an ADR. The 2026-10-13 repo audit (item D2)
flagged the gap, and this record fills it.

Constraints found while migrating:
- At the old 180k context, `coding` ran with under 1 GB free on the 32 GB card. The 3.8
  Q6_K is the same size as 3.6's, so it gains no headroom by itself.
- The Qwen3.8 chat template defaults `reasoning_effort` to `xhigh`. On long build-an-app
  prompts that burned 39,478 tokens, and one run truncated even at a 49k cap.

## Decision
- **`coding` (idx1):** Qwen3.8-27B **Q6_K**, ctx **163840** (down from 184320), q8_0 KV,
  MTP `--spec-draft-n-max 3`. At 160k the card peaks at 30.7 GB of 32 GB (~2 GB free).
- **`big` (idx1+idx2):** Qwen3.8-27B **UD-Q8_K_XL**, 262144 ctx, f16 KV, `-sm layer`, MTP
  `n_max 2`.
  - It migrated as UD-Q6_K_XL on 2026-08-15 and moved to Q8 on 2026-08-16 after a Q6/Q8 A/B
    (`3bb4a70`).
  - Q8 fits at about 51 GB of 64 GB, MTP acceptance is unchanged, and decode is about 5% slower.
  - Quality is at parity with Q6, so Q8 is kept as precision insurance, not a measured win.
- **Both slots are pinned to `reasoning_effort=medium`** via
  `--chat-template-kwargs '{"reasoning_effort":"medium"}'` in `config/llama-swap.base.yaml`.
- `chat` stays Qwen3.6-35B-A3B MoE.
- The Qwen3.6-27B GGUFs stay as a rollback on the bulk-HDD cold tier
  (`models/cold/qwen3.6-27b-mtp/`, `models/cold/qwen3.6-27b/`).

## Consequences
- Positive:
  - **Quality at parity or better.** GSM8K flexible-extract went 0.98 → 0.99 on `coding` and
    held at 0.98 on `big`.
  - The five code evals match 3.6 once sampling is matched. A 3-sample variance study showed
    the two 1-point dips are temperature noise.
  - **MTP still works:** about 2.2–2.4× decode on code at 93–99% draft acceptance.
  - Raw throughput is on par with 3.6.
- Negative / trade-offs:
  - `coding` loses about 20k of context (180k → 160k).
  - `big` Q8 costs about 5% decode.
  - GSM8K strict-match dips to 0.92. That is answer *formatting* under the reasoning
    template, not solve rate.
  - `reasoning_effort` is now a server-side default on both slots.
- Follow-ups / things to watch:
  - Client budgets were re-synced to the smaller `coding` context: `copilot-byok.sh` on
    2026-08-18 (`18d05ea`), and the OpenClaw/pi `contextWindow` on 2026-10-15 (`a031dc6`).
  - The `agentic` mode's `coding` pool now loads the same 3.8 file with MTP off (ADR-0015,
    2026-10-15 addendum).
  - The planned ThinkingCap-Qwen3.6-27B live trial was dropped, because it fine-tunes the
    old base (docs/benchmarking.md, ThinkingCap section).
  - Temperature 1.0 (unsloth's thinking-mode preset) adds a code-break tail risk: one of
    three `big` runs produced a non-parsing program. Prefer temp ~0.6–0.8 for code; that is a
    client-side choice.

## Alternatives considered
- **Stay on Qwen3.6-27B.** Rejected: 3.8 is a drop-in with parity-or-better quality.
- **Keep `coding` at 180k.** Rejected: under 1 GB free left too little OOM margin on a
  full prefill.
- **`big` on UD-Q6_K_XL.** Shipped first and swapped for Q8 a day later (above).
- **`big` on BF16.** Rejected: about 55 GB of weights plus f16 KV overflows 64 GB at 256k.
  The slot ran Qwen3.6-27B BF16 only 2026-07-02 → 07-04.
- **Leave `reasoning_effort` at the template's `xhigh`.** Rejected: long-form codegen became
  unusably slow and truncation-prone.
