Welcome, Adventurer

Beneath the old tavern on Gristle Lane lies a dungeon that has changed hands so many times it practically has a homeowners' association. The previous overlord left in a hurry — unpaid invoices, half-moved treasure, and one very confused garden gnome remain.

You have the deed, a lantern, and questionable judgment. Explore the halls, claim what's yours, and try not to activate anything you shouldn't. Probably.
