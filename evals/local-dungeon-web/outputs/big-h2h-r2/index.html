The Local Dungeon

A tiny text adventure beneath your neighborhood.
Beneath the cracked pavement of Elm Street lies a forgotten warren of stone — the Local Dungeon, where a rusted key, a forgotten lever, and one very old scroll have been waiting for a curious neighbor. The air smells of damp brick and candle wax. Your flashlight battery is dying. Move quickly.
How to Play
lookDescribe the current room.
go northMove (north, south, east, west; "n", "s", "e", "w" also work).
take <item>Pick up an item in the room.
drop <item>Put an item on the floor.
inventoryList what you're carrying ("inv" works).
use <item>Use an item (try the lever).
examine <thing>Look closely at a room or item.
helpShow this list again in the console.
