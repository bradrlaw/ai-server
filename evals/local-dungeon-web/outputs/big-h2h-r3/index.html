The Local Dungeon

Six rooms. Five relics. One very polite dungeon.
Beneath the old mill town of Greymarch lies a dungeon that the locals insist is "only a bit damp and mildly haunted." Its six rooms hold forgotten relics — a rusted key, a lever that opens nothing the blueprint admits to, and a coin worth more than the town's annual harvest. You are the town's newly hired Dungeon Warden. Your first task: explore, collect, and try not to be haunted.
