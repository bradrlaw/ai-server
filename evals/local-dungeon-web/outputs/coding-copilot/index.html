The Local Dungeon

A browser-based text adventure
The Local Dungeon lies beneath a crumbling stone archway you spotted on your morning walk. The air smells of damp earth and forgotten history. Something glimmers faintly in the depths — perhaps lost treasure, perhaps something far older. Your adventure begins the moment you step inside.
How to Play ▼
  • look — Examine your current surroundings
  • go [direction] — Move north, south, east, or west
  • take [item] — Pick up an item in the room
  • inventory — View items you are carrying
  • use [item] — Use an item from your inventory
  • examine [item] — Look closely at something
  • help — Show this command list again
