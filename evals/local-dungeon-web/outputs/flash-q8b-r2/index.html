Welcome, Adventurer

Beneath the old pub on High Street, everyone in town swears there is one dungeon. It has six rooms, a leaky secret door, and treasure that technically belongs to the council. You brought a backpack and a sense of mild curiosity — that should be enough. Explore carefully; the Garden downstairs is said to be very peaceful indeed.
