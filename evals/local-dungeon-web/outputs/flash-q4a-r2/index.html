Beneath the Old Post Office

When the townsfolk renovating the abandoned post office broke through a cellar wall, they found stairs descending far deeper than any cellar had a right to go. Somebody had been down here — recently — and had left in a hurry. You volunteered to explore it, because nobody else would.
