The Local Dungeon

Six rooms, five objects, one quiet ending.

Beneath the Old Chapel

Long before the town was paved over, the masons buried a staircase under the chapel floor and sealed it with a prayer. The stairs still go down. What they go down to is yours to find.

How to Play
  • look / examine [object] — describe the room, or a thing in it.
  • go north / n — move between rooms. Try n, s, e, w.
  • take [item] — pick something up (also: grab, get).
  • drop [item] — set something down.
  • inv — check your pockets (also: inventory).
  • pull [object] / use [object] — work the machinery of the dungeon.
  • read [item] — for things written on.
  • help — show this list again.
  • ↑ / ↓ — scroll through your command history.
  • Click the console or press Skip » to end the typewriter effect instantly.
