The Local Dungeon

A tiny text adventure with modest ambitions and damp walls.

Beneath the village pub lies a dungeon of questionable upkeep.

When the cellar door gave way beneath old Bram the Barman, the village discovered a stairway down into torchlit halls that haven't been swept in a century. Rumors speak of a hoarded treasure, a chamber hidden behind a bookshelf lever, and — worst of all — a genuinely pleasant garden. You volunteered. Nobody else did. Descend, explore, and survive the modesty of it all.
