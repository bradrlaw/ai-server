Welcome, Explorer

Beneath the forgotten streets of the old quarter lies a network of chambers built by a civilization that vanished without a trace. Dust-choked corridors hold rusty locks, hidden levers, and treasures long overlooked. Find your way through the dungeon, uncover its secrets, and discover what lies in the garden beyond.

How to Play
  • look or l — Look around the room
  • go <dir> — Travel north, south, east, or west
  • n / s / e / w — Quick direction
  • take <item> — Pick up an item
  • grab <item> — Synonym for take
  • use <item> — Use an item in your inventory
  • examine <thing> — Look closely at something
  • look at <thing> — Synonym for examine
  • inventory or inv or i — View your items
  • map — See visited rooms on the map
  • help — Show this command list
  • up / down arrows — Browse command history
