The Local Dungeon

A cozy text adventure. Damp walls, dim light, questionable tenants.

The Lore

Beneath the abandoned mill at the edge of town, a dungeon has quietly squatted for three hundred years. Locals swear its rooms rearrange on Tuesdays, that a golden coin glitters somewhere past the armory, and that a hidden chamber sleeps behind the library shelves — waiting for the right lever to be pulled. You have a flickering lantern's worth of courage. Go find what the dark is hiding.

▶ How to Play — Available Commands
  • look — Study your surroundings. look at [thing] / examine [thing] for details.
  • go [direction] — Travel. north, south, east, west — or just n, s, e, w.
  • take [item] — Pick something up (grab and get work too). drop [item] to leave it.
  • use [thing] — Operate mechanisms such as the library lever (pull lever also works).
  • inventory — Check what you're carrying (inv, i).
  • help — Show this command list inside the console.
Tip: press Enter to run a command, Up/Down arrows to repeat old ones, and click Skip (or the console) to speed up the text.
