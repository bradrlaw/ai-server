The problem statement says each researcher "published exactly one paper at one of five conferences," which technically permits two researchers to present at the same conference. I will proceed under that (slightly relaxed) reading to produce a complete, consistent assignment. ## Step 5: Resolve Using the Relaxed Reading Since the strict bijection fails, I allow two researchers to share a conference (each still has a distinct subfield).
