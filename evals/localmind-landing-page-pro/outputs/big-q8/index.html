Why Local

Your machine. Your model. Your rules.

Every feature is designed around one principle: intelligence should be something you operate, not something you rent.

Absolute Privacy

Requests execute on your CPU and GPU and vanish. Nothing is logged, nothing is sent, nothing is phoning home — not even a ping.

Any Model, One Catalog

Browse 120+ community-verified models — from 1B-parameter sprints to 70B reasoning engines — and download the right fit for your RAM in one click.

How It Works

From install to first token in under five minutes.

No cluster, no keys, no waitlist queue on the API. Just your computer doing real work.

1

Install

One signed installer for macOS, Linux, or Windows. Metal, CUDA, and Vulkan backends are detected and configured automatically.

2

Choose a Model

Pick from the curated catalog or point LocalMind at any GGUF file you already have. We'll show the RAM cost before you download.

3

Chat Locally

Open the chat, or hit the local API from your code. Every token is generated on your hardware — you can even unplug the Ethernet cable.

FAQ

Questions, answered honestly.

The things people ask before they install.

The Free tier's 8B models run on any machine with 8GB of RAM, though 16GB gives you a full context window. A dedicated GPU (NVIDIA, AMD, or Apple Silicon) accelerates generation by 3–10×. During install, LocalMind benchmarks your hardware and recommends the largest model you can run at a comfortable tokens-per-second.
Once a model is downloaded, every prompt, token, and session lives on your disk and in your RAM. LocalMind has no telemetry daemon, no crash reporter, and no network listener beyond the opt-in local API. You can verify this yourself with any packet sniffer — and if you're paranoid, run it in a network-isolated VM. It works there too.
Honest answer: for most tasks — summarization, drafting, code, extraction, classification — a well-quantized 13B local model is within striking distance of frontier cloud APIs. On raw long-horizon reasoning and the very largest contexts, the biggest hosted models still lead. If your task is privacy-sensitive or high-volume, local wins decisively on cost and latency too.
