What models can I run on my M3 Max with 64GB of RAM?
LocalMind
Great question! With 64GB of unified memory on your M3 Max, you can comfortably run models up to ~34B parameters at 4-bit quantization. I'd recommend starting with Mistral-Large-Instruct-24B or Llama-3-30B for the best balance of quality and speed. Both will give you 30+ tokens/sec on your hardware.

Can I fine-tune one of these on my own dataset?
LocalMind
Absolutely. LocalMind includes a built-in LoRA trainer. Just upload your dataset in JSONL format, pick your base model, and configure the rank (we suggest r=16 for most tasks). Training a 13B model on 10K examples takes about 45 minutes on your M3 Max. The resulting adapter is only ~200MB.
