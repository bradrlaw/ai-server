FAQ

Questions, answered.

A 7B–8B quantized model runs comfortably on 8 GB of RAM with a modern CPU. For 13B models we recommend 16 GB, and for 30B+ we recommend 32 GB or a discrete GPU with at least 12 GB of VRAM. LocalMind benchmarks your machine on first launch and tells you exactly which models you can run at acceptable speeds.

Yes — inference, prompts, and your document index all live on your disk. The only network calls LocalMind makes are model downloads from the community registry (which you can disable entirely in settings) and anonymous crash reports (also opt-out). We publish the source, so you can audit every byte ourselves.

LocalMind ships with curated builds of Mistral, Llama 3, Phi-3, Gemma, and Qwen in Q4, Q5, and Q8 quantizations — over 200 combinations at launch. Anything in GGUF format also works: drop the file into your models folder and it appears in the picker. Free tier caps at 13B; Pro unlocks 70B and MoE models.

Yes. LocalMind runs an OpenAI-compatible REST API on localhost:11434 with a standard bearer-token auth flow. Anything that speaks the OpenAI SDK — your scripts, LangChain apps, Obsidian plugins, IDE assistants — can point at it by changing the base URL. No code changes required.

Your license is tied to your account, not your hardware. Install LocalMind on the new machine, sign in, and your tier and downloaded model entitlements carry over instantly. Pro licenses allow up to two active machines at a time — handy for a laptop and a workstation.
