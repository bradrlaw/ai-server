FAQ

Questions, answered honestly.

8 GB of RAM gets you a 3B–7B model comfortably. A 16 GB machine with any modern GPU (or Apple Silicon) runs 7B–13B models at reading speed. For 30B+ models we recommend 32 GB+ RAM or 24 GB of VRAM. During setup, LocalMind benchmarks your machine and tells you exactly which models will feel fast — before you download anything.
After installation and model downloads, nothing. No telemetry, no license pings, no crash reporting unless you explicitly enable it. The app ships with a network kill-switch, and our traffic is reproducible with any packet capture tool — a common community test is running Wireshark for a week and finding only the model-download traffic you initiated.
LocalMind's library only lists models released under permissive open licenses — Llama Community, Apache 2.0, MIT, and Mistral Research. We display the license on every model card and verify checksums against the original publisher. You can also paste any Hugging Face repo ID; the license check runs before download begins.
Those are great inference engines, and LocalMind can actually use either one under the hood. What we add is the workflow layer: per-model hardware benchmarks, GPU-accelerated RAG over your local files, hot-swapping models mid-conversation, LoRA fine-tuning without a terminal, and a polished chat experience that non-technical teammates will actually use.
Everything stays yours, because it was never ours. Chats, models, embeddings, and fine-tunes all live in a local folder you own. Downgrading to Free limits concurrent models but never locks you out of history — there is no proprietary cloud format and nothing to "export" because nothing left your disk.
