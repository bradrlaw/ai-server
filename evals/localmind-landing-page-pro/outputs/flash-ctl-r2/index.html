FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

A machine with 16GB of RAM runs 7B–8B models comfortably; 32GB gets you into the 70B range at Q4 quantization. Apple Silicon, NVIDIA (CUDA), and AMD (ROCm) are all supported. On first launch, LocalMind benchmarks your system and tells you exactly which model sizes will feel fast before you download anything.
Any open-weights model in GGUF, MLX, or EXL2 format — that covers Llama 3, Mistral, Mixtral, Gemma 2, Qwen 2, Phi-3, DeepSeek-Coder, and hundreds more. You can browse curated picks in-app or paste any Hugging Face repo ID. New architectures typically get support within a week of upstream releases.
Once a model is downloaded, inference uses zero network access — you can verify this yourself with Little Snitch or your firewall of choice. The only connections LocalMind ever makes are the ones you trigger: model downloads and license checks (at most once every 7 days on paid tiers). There is no telemetry, period.
LocalMind exposes /v1/chat/completions and /v1/embeddings with streaming, function calling, and JSON mode — the same shapes you already code against. Change your base URL to http://localhost:8829/v1 and most apps work unchanged. The differences: no rate limits, no per-token pricing, and your latency depends on your GPU instead of the Pacific Ocean.
Everything stays on your disk. Model files are standard GGUF/MLX formats, and chat history exports to plain JSON or Markdown with one click. If you downgrade to Free, you keep full read access to all your data and can still run any model under 13B — we don't hold your files hostage.
