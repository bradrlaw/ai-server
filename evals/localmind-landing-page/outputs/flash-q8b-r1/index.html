Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

LocalMind is a complete local inference stack — thoughtfully engineered, absurdly fast, and completely yours.

Any Model

Llama, Mistral, Gemma, Qwen, Phi — one-click install for 200+ open-weight models, or import your own GGUF / safetensors files directly.

Zero Latency

No network round-trips. First tokens arrive in milliseconds, and streaming feels instant — because the model lives on your machine.

Full Privacy

Your prompts never leave your device. Ever. No telemetry, no logging, no accounts required. Airplane mode? Still fully functional.

GPU Optimized

Automatic layer offloading across Metal, CUDA, and Vulkan. LocalMind squeezes every last token-per-second out of your silicon.

Our Mission

AI should be personal.

We believe AI should be personal — running on your hardware, answering to you, and answering to no one else. The current model of intelligence-as-a-service means your most private thoughts flow through someone else's data center. LocalMind exists to change that: open-weight models, open-source tooling, and a stubborn conviction that your data belongs at home.
