Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

A local AI engine designed for speed, privacy, and total control over your models.

Any Model

One-click install for Llama, Mistral, Gemma, Phi, and 40+ other open models. Quantized, fine-tuned, or raw — it all runs.

Zero Latency

No network round-trips. Tokens stream directly from your silicon at speeds up to 120 tok/s on modern hardware.

Full Privacy

Your prompts, documents, and conversations never leave your machine. Airplane mode friendly by design.

GPU Optimized

Native kernels for NVIDIA CUDA, AMD ROCm, Apple Metal, and Intel Arc. Automatic offloading when VRAM is tight.
