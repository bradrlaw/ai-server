Any Model

Run Llama, Mistral, Phi, Gemma, and dozens of open-weight models. Switch between them with a single click — no vendor lock-in.

Zero Latency

Responses stream instantly from your own hardware. No network round-trips, no queue times — just raw, local inference speed.

Full Privacy

Your conversations never leave your machine. No telemetry, no data collection, no cloud dependencies. What's yours stays yours.

GPU Optimized

Hardware acceleration for NVIDIA, AMD, and Apple Silicon. LocalMind auto-detects your GPU and tunes inference for maximum throughput.

Install
