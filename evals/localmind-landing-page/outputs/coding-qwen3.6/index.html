Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

LocalMind is built for developers and power users who want the full AI experience without surrendering control.

Any Model

Run Llama, Mistral, Phi, Gemma, or any GGUF/LLaMA model. Bring your own weights or pick from our curated library of 200+ models.

Zero Latency

No network round-trips. No rate limits. Your AI responds at the speed of your hardware — typically under 50ms per token.

Full Privacy

Your conversations never leave your device. No telemetry, no analytics, no data collection. What happens locally stays local.

GPU Optimized

Leverages CUDA, Metal, and Vulkan for maximum throughput. Auto-detects your hardware and tunes quantization for the best performance.
