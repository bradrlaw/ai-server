About

We believe AI should be personal

"The best AI isn't the biggest model in the cloud — it's the one that understands you, runs on your terms, and never sends your thoughts somewhere you didn't ask."

LocalMind was born from a simple frustration: every time we used an AI assistant, we wondered what data was being collected, where it was going, and who could see it. We built LocalMind so you can have world-class AI that lives where it belongs — on your machine.
