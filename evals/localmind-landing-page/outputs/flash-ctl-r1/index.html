Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Built for developers, researchers, and anyone who takes their data seriously.

Any Model

Llama, Mistral, Phi, Gemma, or your own fine-tunes. One-click installs for hundreds of GGUF and safetensors models.

Zero Latency

No network round-trips. Tokens stream the instant they're generated — as fast as your hardware allows, always.
